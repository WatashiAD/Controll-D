Troubleshooting Guide

Phone can't connect or URL won't open

1. Ensure both your PC and phone are connected to the exact same Wi-Fi network.
2. Check firewall: verify port 8765 is open (sudo ufw allow 8765/tcp on Linux).
3. AP Isolation: some guest Wi-Fi networks block device-to-device communication. Disable AP Isolation on your router.

Game or emulator doesn't see the controller

1. Controll-D creates a virtual Xbox 360 controller via /dev/uinput.
2. In emulators (Eden, Yuzu, Ryujinx, RPCS3, Dolphin), go to Controller Settings and select Xbox 360 Controller (Controll-D).
3. On Linux, ensure your user account has read/write permissions to /dev/uinput.

High latency or laggy input

1. Connect your PC to your router with an Ethernet cable for lowest latency.
2. Connect your phone to a 5GHz Wi-Fi band instead of congested 2.4GHz.
3. Keep your phone in clear line-of-sight with your Wi-Fi router.

No vibration on iPhone / iOS Safari

Apple Safari on iOS does not support the Web Vibration API due to operating system restrictions. Haptic vibration works out of the box on Android Chrome, Firefox, and Chromium browsers.
